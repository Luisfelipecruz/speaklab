# Code of conduct

SpeakLab adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
and its enforcement guidelines.

In short: be welcoming and patient, criticise work rather than people, and remember that
some people taking part are writing in a language they are still learning.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@Luisfelipecruz](https://github.com/Luisfelipecruz), through the contact on their GitHub
profile. Reports are read by the maintainer alone and handled in confidence.
